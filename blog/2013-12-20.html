Getting off the ground

While I have been shooting gigapixel photo's for a couple of years now, and have a few pieces on public display, I am just beginning to get the business side of Surround Sight up and running. After a couple of false starts, and a lot of confusion about what to put on the site, who to host with, and how to deal with the huge files that are gigapixel photographs I think I'm on my way. Have a look around the site, don't forget to take a look at the shop, and check back here for updates about upcoming photo shoots, current projects and notes on my process. If you see something that I've done wrong, or know of something that I could do to improve the site, please contact me via the contact page. 

Enjoy!
